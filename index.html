Frequently Asked Questions

What is air duct cleaning and why does it matter?

Air duct cleaning removes dust, pet dander, pollen, and debris that collect inside your ductwork. Clean ducts help improve the air you breathe at home and let your HVAC system move air more easily.

How often should I schedule an air duct cleaning service?

Most homes benefit from air duct cleaning every three to five years. Schedule sooner if you have pets, allergies, recent renovations, visible dust around vents, or signs of mold.

How much does air duct cleaning cost in Jersey City?

We charge a flat rate, quoted before work begins. Call 551-233-0219 and we will give you a clear price based on your property, with no surprise fees afterward.

Is air vent cleaning included with duct cleaning?

Yes. When we clean your ducts, we also clean the vents, grilles, and registers connected to them so dust does not settle right back into your rooms.

How long does air duct cleaning take?

Most residential jobs take about two to four hours, depending on the size of your home and the condition of the ductwork. Commercial jobs vary by building size.
